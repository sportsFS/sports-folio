# Product

## Register

product

## Users

SPORTSFOLIO serves customers shopping for sports gear across cricket, badminton, pickleball, soccer, volleyball, awards, DTF, and jerseys. Users are primarily browsing on mobile, comparing categories quickly, and adding practical equipment to cart with minimal friction.

## Product Purpose

The site is a direct-to-customer storefront for SPORTSFOLIO. It should make the catalog easy to scan, help customers move from sport category to product, and support admin-managed product/order workflows for launch.

## Brand Personality

Sporty, trustworthy, direct. The brand should feel active and retail-ready without becoming noisy or gimmicky.

## Anti-references

Avoid cluttered marketplace pages, generic AI-looking hero sections, mismatched emoji icon systems, inaccessible low-contrast text, and navigation that hides the main shopping paths on mobile.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the sport categories customers recognize first.
- Keep shopping actions obvious, fast, and touch-friendly.
- Preserve product trust with clear names, images, prices, and add-on choices.
- Use brand energy through structure, photography, and restrained accent color rather than decoration.
- Make the admin/customer split invisible to shoppers and reliable for the owner.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Keep touch targets at least 44px, preserve visible focus states, support reduced motion, and avoid relying on color alone for meaning.
